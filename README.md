# Code_lautenschlaeger_et_al_2026

This repository provides the code used to analyze the scATAC-seq data described in Lautenschlaeger et al 2026.

Title: Myocardial infarction induces endothelial chromatin remodeling associated with immune activation and adverse cardiac remodeling

Abstract: 
Despite improved therapies, many survivors of myocardial infarction (MI) progress to heart failure due to adverse cardiac remodeling. Endothelial cells actively coordinate cardiac homeostasis and can undergo injury-induced immunomodulatory transcriptional reprogramming. However, the long-term consequences of this immune reprogramming after MI remain unclear.
Here, we employed epigenomic, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomic profiling to investigate endothelial alterations following MI. We demonstrate that MI induces changes in endothelial cell (EC) chromatin accessibility, resulting in de-repression of the class II major histocompatibility complex transactivator (CIITA), a master regulator of MHC class II expression. This epigenetic reprogramming drives aberrant activation of MHC II–associated immune pathways in cardiac ECs, an important aspect of the immunomodulatory endothelial state. Importantly, endothelial-specific inhibition of Ciita initiated two weeks after MI preserves cardiac function in mice, demonstrating that endothelial immune activation is not merely an epiphenomenon but a mechanistic driver of adverse remodeling.
These findings identify endothelial immune reprogramming as a previously unrecognized contributor to cardiac remodeling post-MI and establish CIITA-dependent pathways as a new therapeutically targetable axis to improve progressive heart failure after infarction.
